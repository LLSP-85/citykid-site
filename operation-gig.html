OPERATION GIG

Ditmas Park porch concerts, all summer long

Tis the season for Ditmas Park’s famously charming porch concerts, brought to you by Operation Gig! Concerts are donation-based, hosted on our neighbors’ lawns and porches here in beautiful Ditmas Park, Brooklyn, featuring and supporting some of Brooklyn’s best working musicians. Bring your own chairs, blankets, and snacks to experience some real summer magic here in the neighborhood.

For the most up-to-date information, check out their Instagram and website, or just check back here: this page updates weekly.

HOW PORCH CONCERTS WORK

  • Neighbors volunteer their porches; Operation Gig books real-deal musicians: jazz, folk, global, and kids’ singalongs.
  • The musicians play the porch. Neighbors can spread out blankets and chairs on lawns and sidewalks, and dancing toddlers are part of the scenery.
  • Most shows run Friday to Sunday, roughly 4–6 PM or 6–8 PM, with the occasional weeknight singalong.
  • Bring cash or Venmo for the musicians; the donations keep Brooklyn’s working artists on the porch circuit.
  • The porches are private homes hosting public shows, so be kind to the lawns.
